# 📝 Blogosphere

Welcome to **Blogosphere** — a modern platform where writers, thinkers, and readers come together to share ideas, stories, and knowledge.

---

## 🌟 Features

- ✍️ **User Auth** — Sign up, log in, and manage your account securely  
- 🗒️ **Write & Publish** — Create beautiful blog posts with a rich text editor  
- 🧭 **Explore Feed** — Discover trending and recent blogs from the community  
- 💬 **Comment & Engage** — Interact with other users through comments  
- 🔍 **Search & Filter** — Easily find content by topic or keyword  
- 🖼️ **Responsive UI** — Fully mobile-friendly and clean design  

---
